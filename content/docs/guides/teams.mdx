title: Teams
description: Collaborate with team members on shared buckets.
---

FlexiBuckets supports team collaboration, allowing multiple users to access shared buckets with controlled permissions.

## Creating a Team

1. Navigate to **Dashboard → Teams**
2. Click **Create Team**
3. Enter a team name and description
4. Select the buckets to share with the team

## Inviting Members

1. Open the team settings
2. Click **Invite Member**
3. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite
4. Select their role

If the person doesn't have a FlexiBuckets account yet, they'll receive an invitation email to create one.

## Team Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|------------|
| **Owner** | Full control: manage members, delete team, all bucket operations |
| **Admin** | Manage members, all bucket operations |
| **Member** | Browse, upload, download, and delete files |
| **Viewer** | Browse and download files only |

## Managing Shared Buckets

Buckets shared with a team are visible to all team members based on their role:

- **Owners and Admins** can add or remove buckets from the team
- **Members** can perform file operations on shared buckets
- **Viewers** can browse and download but not upload or delete

## Team Activity

FlexiBuckets tracks team activity in the centralized [Audit Logs](./audit-logs) system, including:

- File uploads and downloads
- Bucket additions and removals
- Member joins and leaves (`TEAM_CREATE`, `TEAM_JOIN`, `TEAM_LEAVE`, `TEAM_MEMBER_ADD`, `TEAM_MEMBER_REMOVE`)

Audit logs are visible to Owners and Admins at **Dashboard → Audit Logs**.

## Email for Team Invitations

Team invitation emails require email to be configured. See [Email Configuration](../configuration/email) to set up SMTP or Resend. Without email configuration, invitation links are generated but not delivered via email.

## Leaving or Deleting a Team

- **Members** can leave a team at any time from the team settings
- **Owners** can delete a team, which removes all member associations (but does not affect the underlying S3 buckets or files)
- Transferring ownership requires the current owner to assign a new owner before leaving
